# Roadmap

Direction for roughly the next year. Plans can change; this is intent, not a contract.

## Intend to do

- Keep worldstate timers and planning tools accurate and usable as Warframe’s worldstate evolves
- Deepen **codex / item / drop** search and browse quality (coverage, clarity, performance)
- Maintain solid CI, supply-chain hygiene (Scorecard, signing, dependency updates), and contributor docs
- Incremental UX/accessibility and i18n improvements without a full redesign for its own sake

## Not planning (near term)

- Warframe account login, inventory sync, or anything that needs player credentials
- Becoming a general-purpose Warframe wiki replacement
- Monetization or paid “pro” tiers

Feedback and proposals: [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/WFCD/warframe-hub/issues).
